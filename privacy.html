Privacy policy

Breathing Deep: Coldharrow (Android app com.kevinfield.breathingdeep). Effective 24 October 2026.

The game collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends anything over the internet on its own.

What stays on your phone

Your saved game and your settings (volumes, text size, vibration and motion) are stored only on your device, so you can carry on where you left off. The game also keeps a short log of its own errors (the last 20) on your device, to help with problem reports. None of this leaves your phone unless you choose to send it.

If you use Android's own backup, Android may include the game's saved data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is run by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and we cannot see it.

If you contact us

The game's Report a problem and Send feedback buttons open your own email app with a message already filled in. Nothing is sent until you press Send. A problem report can include the game's version, your phone model and Android version, where you were in the story, your last choice, the recent error log and a save code (your game, written as text). You can read and edit all of it before sending.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to reply and to fix problems in the game. We do not share it, sell it or use it for marketing, and we delete problem reports once they are dealt with, or within 12 months.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 16 and over, and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete any email you have sent us. Write to the address below. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If the game ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
